07 / BEHIND THE MIX

Behind the Mix

Like most people, I've spent years listening to and enjoying music without giving much thought to the people, technology, and work behind it.

When my daughter decided to pursue a career in audio engineering, I became curious about the industry she was entering. I wanted to understand it better and find ways to be useful along the way.

The SoundGirls and Women in Vinyl conference offered a great opportunity to learn. I built The Mix Tape to help navigate its extensive program and figured other attendees might find it useful too.

It's a free, independent project. If it helps you, pass it along.

08 / FAQ

A Few Things to Know

Do I still need to select sessions in Zoom Events?

Yes. The Mix Tape helps you plan your schedule, but does not register you for sessions. After choosing your sessions, open the official conference in Zoom Events and add them to your agenda.

What if a session's time or details change?

The Mix Tape uses a snapshot of the conference program, not live updates. Check the official conference agenda in Zoom Events for the latest information.

Can I browse sessions outside my interests?

Yes. Choose Show All Sessions in the tool. Nothing is hidden permanently, and you can add any session to your schedule.

Can I share The Mix Tape?

Yes. Share the website link with another attendee so they can make their own schedule. To keep a copy of your selections, use Print / Save My Schedule.
